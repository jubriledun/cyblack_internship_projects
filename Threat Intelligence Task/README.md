# Threat Intelligence Task

- Based on your organization’s purpose, identify and select the most appropriate sector that represents its operation within OpenCTI. Once identified, carry out threat information gathering and threat landscape analysis on that sector. Your analysis should include:
  - The top threats, actors, or campaigns affecting the sector.
  - Commonly observed malware/tools and techniques (e.g., MITRE ATT&CK TTPs).
  - Patterns of targeting (e.g., preferred victims, initial access methods).
  - Any notable industry alerts, warnings, or historic incidents in the sector.
